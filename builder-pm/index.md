# AI builder PM library

When a customer asks to move an appointment, the assistant can understand the request and still lose the booking. The team needs a way to choose the opportunity, design the service, buy only the dependencies it needs, and prove whether the change helped. These guides follow that decision through a worked service example.

| Decision | Start here | Bring to the review |
|---|---|---|
| Is the opportunity worth a pilot? | [Discovery and baseline](discovery.md) | The current journey, cost of a failure, baseline and owner |
| Which libraries or services earn a place? | [Architecture and procurement](selection.md) | Shortlist, a failure drill and a credible exit path |
| Where does customer data travel? | [Data, retrieval and access](data.md) | Permission map, source revision and deletion exercise |
| What counts as success for the operator? | [Interface and service acceptance](experience.md) | Keyboard review, event replay and assisted handoff |
| Can this workflow pay for itself? | [Unit economics](economics.md) | A worked cost model with attempts, failures and human time |
| Who decides whether to expand? | [Pilot, release and ownership](release.md) | A comparison group, limits, decision date and named owner |
